This setup guide covers the initial installation flow for new users.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download ZXP Installer">
    Download and install [ZXP Installer](https://aescripts.com/learn/zxp-installer/).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install Seiton.zxp">
    Drag and drop the downloaded `Seiton.zxp` file into ZXP Installer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch from After Effects / Premiere Pro">
    Open the Adobe app and choose `Window` > `Extensions` > `Seiton`.

    <Note>
      In Premiere Pro, the extension may appear under `Extensions (Legacy)`.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>
