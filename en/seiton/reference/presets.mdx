---
title: Preset Management
description: Save, load, export, and import Seiton presets.
---

Use presets to reuse folder structures and filter rules. After Effects presets and Premiere Pro presets are stored separately.

## Save

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open presets">
    Click the `Presets` button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a preset">
    Enter a preset name and click `Add`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Load

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select a preset">
    Choose a preset from the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Load">
    Click `Load`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Export / Import

- `Export`: Save the selected preset as a JSON file.
- `Import`: Load a preset from a JSON file.
